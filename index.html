About YT

YUFO TRICKSTER

Contortionist - Handbalancer - BBoy

YuFo Trickster is an accomplished contortionist, B-boy and Brand Ambassador Athlete who has spent his life presenting a concept called “Contortion In Motion”. He blends together different styles and techniques to expose and showcase something extraordinary.

YT (YUFO TRICKSTER) is an internationally recognised performer due to his work with other international artists. As well as he’s request for important events within the UAE ; Celebrations, TV shows, commercial advertisements, films, product launches, private showcases for VIPs, celebrities, and royal families.

Throughout his training in Breakin (Break-Dance), he discovered a new passion for handstands, and has been committed to becoming a well known handstand-coach for more than 5 years. He studied immensely and sought out the guidance of some of the best handstand balancers and practitioners around the world such as; Yuval Aylon, Miguel Sant'ana, and Nicolas.

During the process of learning the art of handstand, he has been perpetually sharing his erudition and edifying of handstands to everyone around the world and loves to learn from others. YT will continue to develop his skills in different aspects and is so excited to experiment with others and learn new and original ideas.

  2. Idea

    After the project plan (which was developed in the initiation phase) has been approved, the project enters the second phase: the definition phase.

  3. Implementation

    The project takes shape during the implementation phase. This phase involves the construction of the actual project result.

  4. Review

    Although it is extremely important, the review phase is often neglected. During this phase, everything is arranged that is necessary to bring the project to a successful completion.

  5. Deliver

    During the final closure, or completion phase, the emphasis is on releasing the final deliverables to the customer.
