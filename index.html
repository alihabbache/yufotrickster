Contortion in Motion

About

Hi, I'm Yufo.

A handstand and flexibility coach who has spent his life exploring a concept called “Contortion in Motion”: strength, balance and range working as one.

Through training in breakin, I discovered a passion for handstands and have been coaching for more than five years. I've studied with some of the best handbalancers in the world, including Yuval Ayalon, Miguel Sant'Ana and Nicolas.

I blend handbalancing, contortion and mobility work into clear progressions, so you build control and range step by step, from your first kick-up to advanced skills.

My background as a contortionist, B-boy and performer across the UAE shapes how I teach. I love sharing what I've learned, learning from everyone I coach, and experimenting with new ideas and collaborators.
